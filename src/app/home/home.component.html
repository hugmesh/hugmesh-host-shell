What is HugMesh?

HugMesh is a self-hosted, federated social platform. Secure Matrix chat, a Mastodon fediverse timeline, discussion forums, a wiki, a video archive and live streaming all run on infrastructure your community controls — under one account.

One account, every service

A single federated identity signs you in to every service on the mesh — no per-app passwords. Your roles decide what you can reach, and administrators can grant or revoke access to an individual service without a re-login.
